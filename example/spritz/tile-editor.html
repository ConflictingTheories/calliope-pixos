Keyboard Shortcuts:
P - Paint tool
E - Erase tool
I - Pick tool
G - Toggle grid
R - Reset camera
ESC - Close dialogs

Camera Controls:
• Drag to rotate camera
• Middle mouse to pan
• Scroll to zoom

Editing:
• Shift + Left Click - Paint selected tile
• Shift + Right Click - Erase tile
• Shift + Drag - Paint/erase multiple cells
• Regular click (Pick tool) - Sample tile
• Hover shows cell coordinates
• Use Edit buttons on tiles/geometry
